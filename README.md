## What you'll find here

### 🚀 [AI-Powered Bid Platform](WIP - Upload soon)
A free enterprise platform that helps bid teams win contracts: a 16-stage pipeline from opportunity registration through to oral delivery and post-bid learning. Every bid makes the next one better.

- **Evidence-grounded generation**: RAG-based drafting of RFP responses, streamed section by section, with confidence scoring on completeness, relevance, tone and risk
- **Structured human review**: assign, annotate and approve sections in a review portal; export is blocked until reviews are resolved
- **Automated compliance checks**: six pre-export checks, including mandatory requirements, so gaps surface before submission rather than hours before it
- **Orals track**: AI-structured presentation decks, anticipated evaluator Q&A, rehearsal tracking with a readiness score
- **Knowledge base that compounds**: semantic search, lessons learned and flagged insights from past bids surface on new ones
- **No lock-in**: bring your own LLM (Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral or Ollama), and swap database, vector store, storage, auth and secrets through configuration, from Docker Compose on a laptop to Azure, Kubernetes or an on-premise deployment
- **Stack**: Python and FastAPI, React and TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Azure AD SSO, Azure Key Vault

**Quick start:** clone the repo, copy the `.env.example` files, run `docker compose up --build`, then open the app and complete the first-run wizard.
